## Why Hidden Tokens?

AI can make everyday work faster. Confidential information should not have to be the price.

Hidden Tokens helps you prepare sensitive documents for AI workflows by replacing selected real-world details with consistent tokens. The AI works with the coded content; the original values stay in your local workflow for restoration.

- **Code before sharing** — replace selected names, identifiers, and amounts with stable tokens.
- **Keep context usable** — consistent tokens let the AI follow who or what is being discussed without seeing the real value.
- **Restore locally** — map tokens back to the original values when you need the result in its real context.
- **Choose what to protect** — use the workflow that fits your document and task.

> Privacy is a workflow decision. Review the content and destination before sharing any document with an AI service.
